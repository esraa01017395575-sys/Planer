# AI Chat Features Documentation

Your AI Coach is designed to be more than just a chatbot. It's an integrated part of your Life OS that understands your goals, tasks, and habits.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Task Management & Subtask Generation
The AI can help you break down complex tasks into manageable subtasks.

- **What happens:** The AI analyzes the task title and suggests a list of logical steps to complete it.

### 2. Long-Term Planning
The AI can help you create structured plans for your goals (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
- **How to use:** In the Plans page, click "New Plan via AI".
- **What happens:** You can describe a goal (e.g., "I want to learn Full-Stack development in 3 months"), and the AI will generate a phase-by-phase roadmap with milestones.

### 3. Contextual Memory (Favorites)
You can send important insights or memories to the AI to revisit them later.
- **How to use:** In the Favorites page, click "Send to AI" on any item.
- **What happens:** The AI uses that specific item as context for the conversation, allowing you to dive deeper into that topic.

### 4. Smart Suggestions
Based on your current schedule and energy levels (set during onboarding), the AI suggests the best times for deep work or rest.
- **How to use:** Look for "Smart Suggestions" in the Chat or Dashboard.
- **What happens:** The AI analyzes your "Peak Energy" times and suggests tasks that match your current state.

### 5. Career Coaching & Discovery
The AI acts as a career coach, helping you identify your strengths and align them with your daily actions.
- **How to use:** Start a conversation in the AI Chat about your career goals.
- **What happens:** The AI conducts a "Discovery & Analysis" session, asking targeted questions to help you build a career roadmap.

## Tips for Better Results
- **Be Specific:** Instead of "I want to be productive", try "I have 2 hours of high energy, what should I focus on from my work tasks?".
- **Use the Context:** The AI knows your language preference (Arabic/English) and your daily rhythm. You don't need to remind it!
- **Feedback Loop:** If a plan or subtask list isn't quite right, just tell the AI to "adjust the plan to be more beginner-friendly" or "add more technical steps".
